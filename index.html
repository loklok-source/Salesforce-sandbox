Before You Start

📖 Instructions

Before opening a practice case, please log in to:

  • Salesforce Live
  • Hive Back Office
  • Salesforce Knowledge Base (KB)

Use these systems throughout the exercise just as you would when supporting a real merchant.

🎯 Training Objectives

  • Become familiar with Salesforce navigation.
  • Retrieve merchant information from Hive.
  • Search and apply the correct KB articles.
  • Follow the correct support workflow.
  • Build confidence before handling live chats.
Important

The AI merchant will not provide the answer. You are expected to investigate using Salesforce, Hive Back Office and the Knowledge Base. Your workflow and communication will be evaluated after each completed case.
